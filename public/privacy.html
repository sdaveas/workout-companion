Privacy policy

Workout Companion — last updated 19 November 2026 · back to the app

Workout Companion is a static web app for logging workouts. It has no account, no sign-up and no backend. Nothing you log is sent to the people who make it.

What is stored, and where

Your programs and workout history are stored in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) on your own device. They stay there until you delete them, clear site data, or export a copy. No server receives them.

There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking scripts, and no cookies used for tracking.

Google Drive (optional)

If you press Save to Google Drive or Load from Google Drive, the app asks Google for permission to access your Drive using the drive.file scope. That scope lets an app see and change only the files it created itself — never the rest of your Drive.

With that permission the app creates one folder, Workout Companion, and writes your backup file inside it. The file belongs to your Google account. The app can read it back on your request. Nobody else can see it, and it is never copied anywhere else.

Access is granted through Google Identity Services. The access token lives in memory for the visit and is never stored. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and you can delete the folder in Drive whenever you like.

Sharing a workout

Share links carry the workout program inside the link itself. Anyone you send the link to can read it. Nothing is uploaded to make that work.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
